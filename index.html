About me

From the database table to the button you click.

I’m a Product Engineer who likes understanding why a feature exists before writing a single line of it.

Most of my day is spent in Java: designing and building REST APIs, writing and tuning SQL, and wiring it all up to Angular front ends. I care about code that is easy to read, APIs that are easy to use, and features that actually solve the problem they were built for.

I studied Computer Science and Business Systems and graduated first in my department. That mix of tech and business is why product work clicks for me. I also use Claude every day to explore ideas, review code and learn faster, and I’ve built with RAG and LangChain.
